Introduction

About Our School

Minuyan National High School is a public secondary educational institution located in Curvada, Barangay Minuyan, Norzagaray, Bulacan, Philippines.

The school provides educational opportunities for students and supports their academic development, personal growth, and responsible participation in the school community.

About the Platform

Our Website

The Web-Based Platform for Accessing School Policies and Guidelines is designed to provide students with an organized and accessible source of school information.

Through this platform, students can browse school policies, understand important guidelines, and stay informed about school announcements.

Our Purpose

To make school policies and guidelines easier for students to access and understand.

Our Goal

To promote student awareness, responsibility, and understanding of school rules and regulations.
